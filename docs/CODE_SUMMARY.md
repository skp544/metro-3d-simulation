3D scene: city geometry is now built from nearby tiles in 1 km chunks; resident triangles vary with the view. Custom landmark footprints exclude duplicate buildings/trees. Trees cap at 6,000, road vehicles at 180 and lamps at 160. Structures remain ≈ 764k triangles (12,976 piers, 9,621 station parts; rails, track bed and parapets ≈ 209k close up; up to 40k instanced sleepers within 900 m). Circulation detail adds about 400 box instances per nearby station, at most six. Landmarks add 108.6k triangles. Trains use 33 instanced meshes and draw models within 5 km of the view, dots further away. DMRC cars are approximately 1.5k triangles each; regional cars approximately 7.8k–9.9k. Formations total 51,520 triangles (RRTS), 32,208 (Aqua), 27,432 (Meerut).

Regional trains now have distinct generic models and six/four/three-car RRTS/Aqua/Meerut formations; DMRC retains its existing six-car representation. Published caps are 160 km/h for RRTS and 120 for Airport Express/Meerut; regular metro/Aqua uses a nominal 60 km/h cap. Shared acceleration/braking profiles drive movement and route estimates; times are estimates, not operator schedules. City coverage now includes all station neighbourhoods and the four landmark districts. Four shared NCRTC stations connect express and local stopping patterns. The five external transfers keep station identities separate.
